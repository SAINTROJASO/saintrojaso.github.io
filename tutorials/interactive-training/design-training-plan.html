Interactive Training Tutorial

2 Design the Training Plan

Before opening Unity, use the interactive planner below to prepare the structure and content of your training.

You can use the planner to:

  • Identify the training format
  • Add scenes and videos
  • Add questions to each scene
  • Write instructions and feedback
  • Identify when instructions and questions will appear
  • Save your progress in the browser
  • Print the completed training plan or save it as a PDF

Note: Your progress is saved only in the current browser and device.
